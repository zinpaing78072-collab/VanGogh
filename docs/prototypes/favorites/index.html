Notes for review

What is real

The shell (rail, header, search, AI toggle), the Liquid Neon tokens, the card ladder and the artwork all come from the app. Movie posters are the committed set; game boxart, album covers and book covers are the ones the earlier detail prototypes used.

What is invented

Notes, tags, collections, saved dates and the taste numbers are sample copy. The four Mixer briefs are hand-written placeholders: there is no model call in these pages.

Open questions

Does Favorites default to the mixed shelf or to collections? Are notes part of the save flow? Does the mixer live here or in the AI search panel? The prototypes are here to make those calls cheaper.
