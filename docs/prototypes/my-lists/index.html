My Lists/Five Directions

Five static browser mock-ups of the My Lists destination, in the app's Liquid Neon skin. Nothing here is wired to the app: no IPC, no backend, no storage. The pages exist to compare layout directions and put possible features on screen before the real slice starts.

The decided model (AGENTS.md, 2026-11-10): a card saves into My Lists — Watchlist and Favorites, across all four content forms. Favorites feed the AI engine. A whole query's saved results go into My Searches, a separate destination. Detail opens from every card, everywhere. Storage will be SQLite. The variants below interpret that model differently, and mark the parts they invent.

01 · Filter-first

The Shelf

The recommended core. One card grid for all four forms, with list and form filters, a library search, sort, and favorite/remove actions on each card. Feels like browsing; scales to the presentation without a new interaction model.

  • Segmented Watchlist / Favorites
  • Form chips + counts
  • Inline card actions
  • Saved date per card

02 · Management-first

The Ledger

The dense read. Smart views on the left, a sortable table on the right, checkboxes that raise a bulk-action bar, compact and comfortable densities. Built for the moment the library is too big to scan as posters.

  • Bulk favorite / watchlist / remove
  • Sortable columns
  • Smart views + counts
  • Compact mode

03 · Insight-first

The Studio

The library as a lens on taste: a bento of counts, a favorites wall, a watchlist queue, the week's saves, and a “draft from my taste” call to action that makes the AI handoff explicit. The strongest story for the presentation.

  • Taste profile + counts
  • Favorites wall
  • In-progress rail
  • Draft-from-library CTA

04 · Tracking-first (speculative)

The Board

Adds a third axis to the saving model: progress. Cards move from Want to through In progress to Finished by drag or arrow, while Favorites stays a star. Tests whether tracking belongs here or belongs to a later slice.

  • Drag between lanes
  • Favorites as a flag
  • Progress bar
  • Status counts
Open the board

05 · Status-first — revision of 04

The Queue

The board's statuses without the three columns: one dropdown switches the whole list between Want to, In progress and Finished. Rows stay horizontal with full-size posters — 2:3 for films, books and games, 1:1 for albums — so the artwork keeps its real shape.

  • Status dropdown + counts
  • Full-size poster rows
  • Favorite / advance / remove
  • Form filter + favorites-only
Open the queue

Decided — do not re-litigate

  • One library across Movies, Music, Books, Games.
  • Watchlist + Favorites, saved from any card.
  • Favorites feed the AI engine.
  • Detail opens from every card.
  • My Searches is a separate destination.
  • SQLite is the storage answer from day one.

Speculative — proposals only

  • Progress statuses (Want to / In progress / Finished).
  • Custom named lists, beyond Watchlist + Favorites.
  • Bulk export and arbitrary sorting.
  • Saved dates, personal notes, “why I saved it”.
  • Continue-where-you-left-off progress in percent.
  • Draft-from-library as a first-class AI action.

Implementation seam

A real slice needs a library store and a command pair that follows the Windows rule (synchronous UI thread, IO-pool work behind a generation): load the saved rows, then mutate them. The four rendered cards already exist; the saved item adds lists, savedAt, and an identity the detail request understands. The prototypes intentionally never touch window.krema.
